# Philosophy

Astrology Engine supports careful astronomical computation and culturally aware
exploration of symbolic traditions. People retain their sovereignty. A computed
longitude is astronomical data; its meaning in a tradition is an interpretation.
The engine does not treat symbolic compatibility or forecasting as measured human
outcomes. Modern Norse correspondences remain an optional, explicitly modern layer.

We value reproducibility, open knowledge, privacy, accessibility, and historical
curiosity. Preserve disagreements between schools rather than flattening them.
Do not invent astronomical results, lost historical knowledge, universal rules,
or medical/financial guarantees. Unknown inputs and unsupported methods deserve
clear output. A small verified capability is preferable to a large misleading one.
