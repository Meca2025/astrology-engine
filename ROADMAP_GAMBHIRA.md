# Roadmap — Gambhira: the Vedic Depths

The Western, runic, Chinese and Tibetan arts stand complete. The
Vedic wing of the engine has dashas, vargas and panchanga — but
not yet the strength systems, the great combinations, or the
Jaimini school. This program digs those wells, slice by slice,
with Mythic Engineering (Skald → Rúnhild → Eldra → Sólrún →
Védis → Scribe), task doc pushed before code, Sólrún subagent
verification per slice, push with verified remote HEAD each time.

## Slices

- **G01 — Yoga engine**: the great combinations computed from
  D1 (and D9 where classical): Pancha Mahapurusha, Gaja Kesari,
  Budha-Aditya, Dhana yogas, Raja-yoga basics, Kemadruma and
  Sakata. Classical definitions cited (BPHS, Phaladeepika);
  computation separate from the interpretive weight the
  tradition gives them.
- **G02 — Shadbala**: the sixfold strength (Sthana, Dig, Kala,
  Chesta, Naisargika, Drik) in Rupas/Virupas, with honest notes
  wherever the tradition's finer corrections are approximated.
- **G03 — Ashtakavarga**: benefic points planet-by-planet and
  the Sarvashtakavarga total, with the classical transit-reading
  method documented.
- **G04 — Jaimini foundations**: the seven karakas, Arudha Pada,
  and Chara dasha — the other great school, clearly labeled as
  such, never mixed silently with Parashari timing.
